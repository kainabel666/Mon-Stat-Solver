Monster stat solver

Enter the stats you want a monster to have in game. The page works back through the monlvl.txt L- columns (used by single player and TCP/IP games) to the numbers monstats.txt needs. Files are read in your browser and never uploaded.

Data folder

Select your mod's data folder, or drop it here. The page reads the .txt files from global/excel and the .json files from local/lng/strings. Your browser asks once to let the page view the folder; only the files listed below are opened, and nothing is uploaded.

      How the numbers are worked out

      In game, each stat is the monstats.txt value times the monlvl.txt factor for the monster's level, divided by 100 with the remainder dropped. HP uses L-HP, Defense L-AC, Experience L-XP, attack rating L-TH, and all damage, elemental included, L-DM. Nightmare and Hell use the (N) and (H) columns.

      For each target the page picks the smallest monstats value that produces it. At high levels a factor above 100 skips some values; those targets get the nearest reachable value and show in orange. Targets that already match the file keep the file's own number.

      In the expansion, regular Nightmare and Hell monsters take the area's level from levels.txt (MonLvlEx(N), MonLvlEx(H)), not Level(N) or Level(H). In Normal they use monstats.txt Level, unless the game is patched to use the area level there too; tick "Normal uses area level" if yours is. Load levels.txt and pick an area to fill these in, or type the levels yourself. Monsters with boss set keep their own level everywhere.

      Each El slot's type decides how its rows work. Duration (ElDur, in frames, 25 per second) only applies to cold, poison, stun, burning and freeze, plus random, which falls back to 25 frames when it's 0; for other types the row is locked and Apply clears the file's value. Poison follows the engine's special case, and burning is calculated the same way: the level-scaled damage times 10 is the poison rate in 1/256ths of a point per frame, and the duration is doubled. The poison target is the total the game shows, rate × frames ÷ 256, so a duration of 100 with 5 scaled damage deals 5 × 10 × 200 ÷ 256 = 39 over 8 seconds. Changing a poison or burning duration keeps its target total and adjusts the damage input.

      These are base monster stats. Champion and unique level bonuses and HP multipliers, player count scaling, and poison or cold durations are applied on top by the game. Monsters with noRatio set take their stats from the skill that summons them, so they can't be edited here.
